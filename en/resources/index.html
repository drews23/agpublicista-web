Templates you can try before downloading

Explore three HTML, CSS, and JavaScript templates: an interactive resource carousel, glass social buttons, and layered social buttons. Each English ZIP includes local fonts, an English README, and license notices.

Choose the format your project needs

  • SVG and PNG icons: choose SVG for scalable, editable vectors or PNG for an application that needs raster images. Styles, dimensions, and quantities vary by pack.
  • WAV sound effects: compare the inventory and demonstration in each guide. The 68-effects library spans six categories and is not a single archive.
  • After Effects projects: review the original tutorial and any stated font or dependency requirements before opening a copy of the project.
  • Spline scenes: editable .spline files require Spline. The original Spanish guides explain web integration and player dependencies.
  • Web templates: HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with live previews and separate downloadable ZIPs.

Free access from each guide

Lienzo does not require an account or email to access these resources. YouTube hosts demonstrations and tutorials. Packs linked to MediaFire are hosted by that provider, which may display its own advertising or download steps. Web-template ZIPs are served directly from Lienzo. Preparation, credits, and usage terms

Preparation varies by resource: organized icon packs, documented SVG optimization, audio inventories and samples, customization tutorials, and integration examples. Size reductions reported in an individual guide apply to that pack, not the entire catalog.

Credits distinguish collaboration with MD Estudio Creativo, distribution by Lienzo, and applicable third-party licenses. Read the usage terms, credits and third-party licenses, and the individual guide. A free download does not grant permission to resell or redistribute it as another pack.

If you believe a file infringes your rights, email contacto@lienzo.tools with the article and filename. I remove the material while reviewing the case, within 48 to 72 hours.
