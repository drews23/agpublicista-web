SVG or PNG?

Use SVG for scalable print layouts and editable website graphics. Check every symbol at its actual display size: vectors stay sharp, but fine details can still become hard to recognize. Use PNG when your email editor or publishing system does not accept SVG. For a 48 px display on a double-density screen, use the 96 px export and set its visual width to 48 px.

For a larger image, export the SVG at the required size instead of enlarging the 96 px PNG. For print, check the output color profile and make a physical proof.

Match your restaurant's colors

Open a copy of the SVG in a vector or text editor and inspect its fill values. For reusable website colors, use a CSS mask rather than exporting a new file for every category. See the SVG color guide (Spanish).

Questions before downloading

Are the light and dark variants different drawings?

No. They retain the same drawing and change its color for different backgrounds. Check contrast on your final background.

Can I use the icons in a restaurant project?

Personal and commercial projects are covered by the applicable conditions. Keep individual notices; do not resell or redistribute the collection as another pack.

Does the pack include 512 px PNG files?

No. This pack includes PNGs at 24, 48, and 96 px. Export the SVG if you need a larger raster image.
