      What it checks, and what needs your judgment

      The studio checks asset counts, lengths, URLs, duplicates, pinning, and selected literal matches. It does not certify policies, brand use, availability, or results. A review with no errors does not guarantee approval or performance.

      Ad text is processed in your browser and is not saved automatically. This version has no Google Ads account connection or AI generation.
