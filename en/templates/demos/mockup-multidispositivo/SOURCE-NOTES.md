## Generación del portátil / Laptop generation

ImageGen integrado, con transparencia activada. La pantalla verde de registro queda cubierta por la capa editable del mockup. Built-in ImageGen with transparency enabled. The editable screen covers the green registration area.

> Generate one production-ready photorealistic laptop product mockup asset on a truly transparent background. Wide landscape image, one entire generic unbranded premium 14-inch space-gray laptop, open approximately 100 degrees, centered and rigorously front-on, with the camera optical axis perpendicular to the DISPLAY so all four display edges are straight, parallel, level and symmetric. Do not tilt the image in either axis and do not use a three-quarter side angle. Slight visible keyboard deck (only the nearest 7–10 percent of device height), restrained realistic foreshortening. Display aperture 16:10, completely flat uniform bright green (#00ff00) chroma key from edge to edge of the active pixels, with no glare, text, imagery, gradients or reflections in the green area; straight edges and very slightly rounded corners. Keep physically realistic thin charcoal glass bezel surrounding green rectangle, tiny webcam dot in bezel, smooth narrow dark hinge, a realistic machined dark-silver aluminum body with fine brushing and tactile softly lit edges, individual subtle keyboard keys, subtle front lip and real product photography lighting. Studio packshot precision, realistic scale relationships and physical materials. Full object visible with small transparent safety padding around all edges. No scene or floor, no visible background, no text, no company marks, no accessories, no watermarks, no stickers, no drawings, no illustration. This is a photograph-grade raster shell for an interactive web mockup; the user will cover the green panel with their own content.
