# Perspective Laptop Mockup · Lienzo

Show a screen recording or screenshot on a graphite laptop. Drag with a mouse or finger to orbit 360° and change the viewing height. Use the arrow keys to orbit and Home or the reset button to return to the starting angle. Devices without WebGL 2 show a fixed perspective.

1. Extract the template ZIP and open `index.html`.
2. Open **Settings** or select **Change screen content**. In **Screen media**, upload an MP4 up to 40 MiB or a PNG, JPG, or WebP up to 8 MiB. Your browser reads the files on your device.
3. Use **Screen fit** to show the whole file or fill the screen. Play the video with the controls below the laptop. The laptop stays in view as you scroll through settings. Open **Sample text and color** or **Your brand** to edit those details.
4. In **Export**, choose **Download project ZIP** to include your video and settings. Extract that ZIP and open `index.html` for offline playback. With a still image, you can also save the HTML alongside the template files.

If your browser cannot play an MP4, try an H.264 export. Use material you can share if you plan to send the ZIP to someone else. See `SOURCE-NOTES.md`, `LICENSE-LIENZO.txt`, and the font notices in `assets/`.
