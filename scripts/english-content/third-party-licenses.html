Why this page exists

Open licenses commonly require copyright notices to travel with code. Lienzo preserves those notices and also makes the credits visible here. Links point to original projects or license texts, rather than presenting our explanations as replacements. The Spanish source records its link check on August 31, 2026; that is a historical date, not a fresh verification of every external link.

This differs from the Lienzo License: that explains your use of downloads and tool output; this page explains the works Lienzo uses and their permissions.

Software

These two MIT-licensed libraries run in the browser and do not upload tool input.

SVGO · MIT

Kir Belevich and contributors. Version 4.0.2, bundled for browsers. The engine for the SVG optimizer (Spanish). Files are optimized locally. Project · Full license.

qrcode-generator · MIT

Kazuhiko Arase. Version 1.4.4. Computes the module matrix for the QR generators (Spanish). Drawing, colors, export, and content formats—Wi-Fi, vCard, email, and SMS—are Lienzo code built on top. A tested library avoids errors in correction tables that could create unscannable codes. Project · Full license.

Design and interface

These MIT-licensed works provided starting points. Lienzo adapted them to its identity and changed performance or accessibility behavior as described.

Eclipse Hero · MIT · Rafa

Original work. Basis for the Spanish homepage's particles and disk. The canvas fits its section rather than the window, respects prefers-reduced-motion, and pauses when the hero is offscreen or the tab is hidden.

Neon Glass Context Menu · MIT · themrsami

Original creator. Visual vocabulary for neon-glass cards and panels. Adapted to violet, turquoise, and amber. Background blur is applied from 768 px; mobile cards remain opaque to reduce scrolling cost.

Neon Glass Card · MIT · NANOUU

Original creator. Basis for recent-resource cards on the Spanish homepage. Adapted with brand colors, actual pack-file visuals, and a custom marquee that pauses on hover.

Bento element lab · MIT · Mr Abu Hani

Original creator. Conceptual starting point for the CSS gallery (Spanish). Rewritten: the 60 recipes, their code, and content are original to Lienzo. The category organization idea was retained; copying each component's code was not a feature of the original.

Animated Glassmorphic Social Buttons · MIT · Sabiha Samha

Author as stated in the original ZIP license. Original work. Basis for glass social buttons. Lienzo adapted colors, text, links, local SVG icons, visible focus, accessible labels, reduced motion, and mobile layout; corrected duplicated HTML and the stylesheet path. The complete original MIT text and license for Lienzo's contributions remain in the ZIP.

Glassmorphism social media button · MIT · Nick

Author as stated in the original ZIP license. Original work. Basis for layered social buttons. Lienzo adapted palette, content, local SVGs, keyboard states, reduced motion, and responsive layout. The original MIT notice remains intact in the ZIP.

The collection's resource carousel is Lienzo's original code. It does not include code or images from the external Swiper ZIP.
