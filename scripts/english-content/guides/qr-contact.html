Create a contact QR code that carries the actual details

This tool encodes a vCard 3.0: a structured name, phone number, email, company, job title, and website. A compatible scanner can offer to create a contact. The recipient reviews the fields and confirms the save; scanning does not silently add someone to an address book.

Contact card or website link?

Choose this contact QR when you want the details inside the code. Choose a URL QR code when you want to open a portfolio or contact page you can update. An embedded vCard is static: changing this form does not change a printed code or contacts someone has already saved. A URL pointing to your own maintained page can keep the same address while the page changes.

Build and check your card

  1. Enter a recognizable name and the contact details you want to share. The preview can start with a first name, last name, phone, or email; a useful finished card should include a name and a way to reach you. Company, title, and website are optional.
  2. Use an international phone number when you expect international contacts. Check the email and include https:// in the website address. This form does not verify that the number or address belongs to you.
  3. Keep the code dark on a light background. If you add a logo, the tool selects error correction H; keep the quiet border clear.
  4. Download PNG for a raster image or SVG for a scalable layout. These downloads are images of the QR code, not a separate .vcf contact file.
  5. Scan the downloaded image and a printed proof. Compare the name, phone, email, and website in the contact preview before confirming.

An example you can try

Use the fictional name Alex Rivera, company Example Studio, and email alex@example.com. Leave the other fields blank. The scanner should receive those three entered values. This example is for testing, not a real contact or a testimonial.

Will it work on every iPhone and Android phone?

Reader support varies. Some camera apps offer a contact action, while others show the encoded text or need another QR reader. Test on the devices your recipients use. A successful browser check does not certify every camera or print condition.

Can I include several phone numbers or update an existing contact?

This form has one phone and one email field. It does not merge contacts, synchronize changes, or manage a hosted digital business card. To change the embedded details, generate a new QR image and replace the old one.

Who can read the information?

QR generation and logo processing happen in your browser. Anyone who receives the QR image can decode its contact details; share only information you intend to make available.
