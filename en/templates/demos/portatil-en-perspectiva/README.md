# Perspective Laptop Mockup · Lienzo

Show a screen recording or screenshot inside an angled graphite laptop. The screen keeps its perspective as you resize the window.

1. Extract the template ZIP and open `index.html`.
2. Select **Change screen content** and choose an MP4 up to 40 MiB. For a still image, choose a PNG, JPG, or WebP up to 8 MB. Your browser reads the files on your device.
3. Choose whether to show the whole file or crop it to fill the screen. Use the playback controls below the laptop to test the video.
4. In **Export**, choose **Download project ZIP** to include your video and settings. Extract that ZIP and open `index.html` for offline playback. With a still image, you can also save the HTML alongside the template files.

If your browser cannot play an MP4, try an H.264 export. Use material you can share if you plan to send the ZIP to someone else. See `SOURCE-NOTES.md`, `LICENSE-LIENZO.txt`, and the font notices in `assets/`.


## Stable editing workspace

Open Settings to keep the preview beside the form. Apply commits content changes; Cancel discards unapplied fields. Hiding Settings preserves those fields. Image and motion controls update immediately and are labeled separately. Keep editor-workspace.css and editor-workspace.js beside the HTML, including when using a downloaded edited HTML.


## Your brand

Brand changes apply immediately and are independent of content Apply/Cancel. Local font uploads stay in the browser and are embedded in exported HTML. Use only fonts you have permission to distribute. Keep template-brand.css and template-brand.js beside full-studio HTML; individual scene/panel exports embed their current brand styles.
