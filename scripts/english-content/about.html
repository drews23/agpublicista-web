Andrés García, Advertising Professional and Art Director

I'm a web designer, advertising professional, and art director focused on branding, digital strategy, and useful creative content. Lienzo is where I share resources, practical tools, and the thinking behind the work.

What I do

I hold a degree in Advertising from UCES in Buenos Aires and have spent more than a decade working where design and strategy meet: visual identity, branding, web design, art direction, social media, and motion graphics. I like to consider a project from its initial concept through to the final published file, keeping the brand's message consistent.

My work started with integrated marketing strategy and has moved closer to the practical decisions that support a client's business. I lead MD Estudio Creativo, working with brands on digital strategy, positioning, conversion optimization, and applied AI agents for specific industries.

Alongside client work, I share what I learn through Lienzo on YouTube: real design processes, downloadable templates and packs, and techniques you can use in your own projects.

What you'll find on Lienzo

Lienzo extends the channel with a practical toolbox for creators, designers, and entrepreneurs. The original content is in Spanish; this independent English edition makes the translated guides and tools easier to explore.

  • Free browser tools, including SVG favicons and animated wavy text. Create and copy the result without registering.
  • Downloadable resources: sound effects, After Effects templates, icons, and material accompanying channel videos.
  • Guides and tutorials explaining how to use the resources and make practical design decisions.
