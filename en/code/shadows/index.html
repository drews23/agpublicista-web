Use layers to control depth
Choose box-shadow or text-shadow and add up to five layers. Box shadows support spread and inset; text shadows do not. The first listed shadow is painted on top. The preview background and box radius help you judge the result, but are not included in the exported shadow rule.
Why does the shadow look different on my site?
Background color, box shape, and neighboring elements affect the perceived depth. Preview both light and dark surfaces and verify the result in your real layout.
