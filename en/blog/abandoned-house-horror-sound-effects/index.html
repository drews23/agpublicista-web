How to use these effects in a horror scene

Build tension with ambience

Keep the first WAV low as a bed under the scene. Leave a short pause before the second set of knocks to give the tighter shot more weight.

Give each sound a place

Roll off some high frequencies and turn down distant knocks to suggest something offscreen. In the short film, the slam lands as the iron door shuts.

Let the tail ring out

The door slam feels heavier when its resonance can decay. If you need a shorter tail, try a short fade and compare it with the original in Audio Studio.
