Choose and mix a horror sound

  1. Anticipation. Try a “Terror inquietante” variant at a moderate level before the visual reveal. Lower it during dialogue and let it emerge in the silence afterward.
  2. Appearance. Place a “Grito - Fantasmas” when the image supports the scare. Align the WAV's attack to the reveal frame; moving it one or two frames can change the perceived synchronization.
  3. Exit. Listen to the file's ending before trimming. A short fade can prevent an abrupt cut, but an already natural ending may need no added fade.

Check the mix on headphones and a phone speaker. Lower the effect if it masks speech or clips the master. In Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, CapCut, or another WAV-compatible editor, keep effects on their own track so timing and level can be adjusted without altering the original file.
