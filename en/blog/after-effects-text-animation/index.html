Free After Effects Text Animation Templates and Editing Guide

Original guide published September 9, 2026. Source updated September 14, 2026. English edition October 10, 2026 · By Lienzo.

Give titles a readable rhythm without building every keyframe from scratch. This kinetic typography pack for Adobe After Effects includes bold title entrances, word-by-word reveals, and animated cursor treatments. The original Spanish tutorial walks through fonts, colors, duration, cursor color, and export.

What's in the pack

  • Strong title entrances for a main announcement, section opening, or important figure.
  • Word-by-word reveals for a phrase that develops over time.
  • Animated cursor text for a typing-style treatment.

The archive contains one project named after effect text animation pack.aep. Inspect font, effect, and version warnings when opening it. This page does not establish an untested minimum version or guarantee that no plugins are needed.

Download the text animation pack

  1. Open the original MediaFire file using the download button.
  2. Download the RAR and extract it.
  3. Keep the original project and open a working copy in After Effects.
  4. Replace the text and choose your fonts and colors before adjusting duration.

Where kinetic typography helps

Use a short animated title for a section change, word-by-word text to support a voiceover, or a cursor treatment in a software demo. For Reels and TikTok, test the message with audio muted so the text remains understandable on its own. Keep important figures or deadlines separate from longer paragraphs.

Movement should support hierarchy. A sound cue from the sound effects library can reinforce an entrance; it should not compete with speech. If you need a website treatment instead of a rendered video, explore the typography tool (Spanish) and animated wavy text tool (Spanish).

Exercise: one message in six seconds

These are suggested edit timings, not preset durations promised by the pack:

  • 0–2 seconds: “Give your titles rhythm.” Present one idea and let the entrance finish.
  • 2–4 seconds: “Change text and color.” Leave a stable reading interval. Split an overlong phrase before shrinking it too far.
  • 4–6 seconds: “Try the free pack.” Keep the closing message readable and clear of the cursor.

Export a test and watch it on a phone without audio. If you need to pause, extend the stable interval or shorten the message. Then check the voiceover version: text should not reveal information before the speaker explains it.

No. The project contains existing animations to customize. Replace the text and adjust fonts, colors, and timing while following the original tutorial.
