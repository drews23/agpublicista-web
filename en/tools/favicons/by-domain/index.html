Choose, customize, and test your favicon
Browse the gallery or create an emoji or monogram icon. Copy the generated link tag into the head of your HTML page. Test the result on your actual website: browser caches can delay favicon updates. Domain lookup contacts external services; those services may return cached icons or fallback images.
Will every emoji look the same?
No. Emoji glyphs depend on the operating system and installed fonts. A PNG captures this device’s rendering; an SVG that contains text can still vary on another device.
