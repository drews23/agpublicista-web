Make the template fit your brand

Replace the text

Edit the title's text layer. Check font size, line breaks, and safe margins. Review the entrance and exit too: a word that fits when stationary can be cropped during movement.

Adjust the colors

Look for composition color controls first. Change one and inspect which layers respond. If some elements keep their original color, inspect their fills and effects separately. Check the palette over the actual footage.

Adjust the timing

For proportional timing changes, scale the selected keyframes together. For larger changes, review the precomposition's time-stretch behavior. After any change, check motion blur, the start and end of the transition, and whether the frame remains fully covered.

Add a sound cue only where it supports the cut. Explore the sound effects library, or use the dedicated text animation template for kinetic typography.

A short test before the full render

  1. Set the target framing and replace a title with “Make it your own.” Check its margins after the entrance.
  2. Place the transition between the title card and a screen recording. Step through the cut to find any uncovered or black frame.
  3. Render that section. Check fonts, transparency, edges, and the final frame before repeating the treatment across the video.

This is an editing exercise, not an additional composition promised in the download. For vertical delivery, reposition titles and elements; changing only composition width and height may crop the animation. Check your computer against the official Adobe system requirements. The AEP's file size does not determine preview memory needs.
