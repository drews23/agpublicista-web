# Expanding Panel Gallery — Lienzo

Five photographs of a valley, a forest, dunes, a museum, and a coastline. Subtle motion shows how to present a collection, portfolio, or visual story. Replace the sample content before publishing your project. Native HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; no installation, account, CDN, API, or third-party libraries.

## Get started

Extract the complete ZIP and open index.html. Click or tap a panel. Titles stay identifiable when panels are collapsed. You can also use the five indicators, Previous, and Next.

Under **Settings → Gallery layout**, Responsive chooses the orientation for the available space. Horizontal and Vertical keep your preference when you resize. Hide Settings to see the full gallery. On a phone, horizontal mode scrolls within the gallery, and vertical mode scrolls with the page.

With a panel selector focused, use Left/Right in horizontal mode or Up/Down in vertical mode. Home and End select the first and last panels. **Open on hover** works when Settings is closed and requires a mouse or trackpad. Expand photo opens a larger view with its own navigation, pause, and complete description. Escape closes it and returns focus.

## Customize each panel

Open **Settings** and choose a **Panel to edit**. Scroll through the controls while the selected panel stays in view. **Text and link** edits the title, category, description, HTTP(S) link, and accent. Select **Apply changes** before switching panels. Reset item affects only that panel.

Under **Image**, add a PNG, JPEG, or WebP up to 8 MB. Your browser processes the photo on your device. Add alt text when the photo conveys information. **Restore sample photo** brings back the initial photograph for that panel.

New photos use **Full image · no crop** to keep every edge and preserve the aspect ratio. **Fill 6:5 · adjustable crop** shows position and zoom controls and enables motion. The panel, expanded view, and standalone HTML keep the same framing.

Sample photos fill the panels. When you upload your own photo, the text appears in a separate area.

Under **Motion**, Intensity changes the depth and Cycle duration changes the pace. Set Intensity to zero for a still panel. Only the active panel animates. Manual pause, reduced motion, dialogs, and leaving the viewport suspend the effect.

## Keep and integrate your work

Settings live temporarily in memory. Reloading resets the example.

- Under **Export**, choose **Download gallery** to keep all panels, photos, and applied settings. Place the HTML beside editor-workspace.css, editor-workspace.js, studio.css, studio.js, component.css, component.js, and assets/ from the ZIP.
- **Download current panel** saves a standalone HTML file with its photo, styles, fonts, and notices. It includes text, links, framing, and the pause control when there is motion. You can open it offline.

Keep license notices and accessibility features when integrating. The hosted demo uses noindex; downloads omit it. Set your own metadata before publishing. Sample links lead to Lienzo and should be replaced with the real destination for each story.

## Stable editing workspace

Open Settings to keep the preview beside the form. Apply commits content changes; Cancel discards unapplied fields. Hiding Settings preserves those fields. Image and motion controls update immediately and are labeled separately. Keep editor-workspace.css and editor-workspace.js beside the HTML, including when using a downloaded edited HTML.


## Your brand

Choose your own three accent colors in Your brand. Optional surface/text colors and display/body fonts apply only to the reusable piece, while the authoring interface keeps Lienzo identity. Brand changes apply immediately and are independent of content Apply/Cancel. Local font uploads stay in the browser and are embedded in exported HTML. Use only fonts you have permission to distribute. Keep template-brand.css and template-brand.js beside full-studio HTML; individual scene/panel exports embed their current brand styles.
