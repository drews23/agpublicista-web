Strategy, creativity, and operations

I hold a bachelor's degree in Advertising from Universidad de Ciencias Empresariales y Sociales (UCES) in Buenos Aires. My background spans more than a decade of work in Colombia, Argentina, and Brazil, covering brand strategy, campaigns, content, and digital development. I work from Bogotá in Spanish and English.

I'm the founder and strategy director of MD Estudio Creativo. There, I connect positioning, visual identity, advertising, and web design with what happens after the click: customer service, lead generation, scheduling, and follow-up. I develop websites, applications, and AI agents integrated with business operations.

That approach also shapes Lienzo: I want an idea to become something you can actually use. On Lienzo on YouTube, I share design and editing workflows. This site brings together the tools, templates, downloads, and explanations that accompany those videos.

For a closer look at my professional background and education, see my digital resume.

Education and tools

I studied Advertising at UCES from 2015 to 2020. Before that, I studied Graphic Design at Universidad Jorge Tadeo Lozano from 2007 to 2009 without completing the program. My additional training includes Google Ads Display, Google Analytics, and an EF SET English certificate, as listed on my resume.

I combine creative direction, branding, and motion graphics with SEO, user experience, and analytics. Depending on the project, I work with Next.js, Astro, Flutter, and Firebase, along with the WhatsApp and Instagram APIs and AI models to build automated workflows.
