Which pack fits the edit?

  • Visible cut: digital cuts or glitch.
  • Camera or object movement: whooshes or transitions.
  • Action result: game sounds.
  • Anticipation or reveal: suspense followed by an impact.
  • Brand entrance or exit: intro and fade sounds.

Always listen in the full mix. An isolated sample helps compare character; voice, music, and ambience determine whether it works in the finished video.

A practical workflow

  1. Mark the cuts, movements, and actions that need emphasis.
  2. Choose a sound family and try two candidates at each marker.
  3. Keep effects on tracks separate from voice and music.
  4. Align the attack or impact with the image, not automatically the beginning of the file.
  5. Adjust level and tail. Check headphones and a phone speaker.

Before bringing an effect into your edit, adjust its level and fades in Lienzo Audio Studio. For a quick selection, use Trim audio. Then import the result into your video editor and align the accent with the picture; these audio tools do not edit your video timeline.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I download the effects?

Use buttons in this guide or the specific pack articles. YouTube hosts demonstrations; Lienzo provides download access, instructions, and usage conditions.

Do they work in Premiere Pro, CapCut, and DaVinci Resolve?

Yes. They are independent WAV audio files. Import them on a separate track to control timing, level, and fades.

How do I trim a WAV sound effect without losing the impact?

Find the impact on the waveform and leave a little room before it. Shorten the tail first, then add a short fade-out in Audio Studio if the cut clicks. Export the WAV and line up the impact with the cut in your video editor.

Can I use them in monetized videos?

Yes, in personal and commercial projects under the linked conditions. Reselling or redistributing the files as another pack is not permitted.
