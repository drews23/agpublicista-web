Clear answers before you export.

Is my audio uploaded to a server?

No. This editor decodes and processes audio in your browser. Exports are created on your device. Site services, including advertising and consent, follow their own privacy policies.

Which formats can I open?

MP3, WAV, M4A/AAC, OGG/Opus, and FLAC when your browser supports the source codec. If decoding fails, the editor shows a message. A file extension alone does not guarantee compatibility.

Does changing speed preserve pitch?

No. Speed and pitch change together through playback rate. This editor does not provide pitch-preserving time-stretching.

Does normalization prevent all distortion?

Normalization adjusts the selection peak to −1 dBFS. It cannot repair existing distortion and does not measure LUFS loudness. If you increase gain afterward, check the peak again and listen to the output.

Should I choose 16-bit or 24-bit WAV?

WAV stores uncompressed PCM audio. 16-bit is often suitable for sharing; 24-bit can be useful for further editing. Exporting at a higher bit depth cannot recover detail missing from the original.

What are the editor limits?

Uploads are limited to 100 MiB per file. Decoded PCM audio is limited to 384 MiB, and estimated working memory to 768 MiB. These are preventive limits, not a guarantee that every device can finish an operation. Your browser may need additional memory for decoding; keep the original and use shorter recordings on devices with limited memory.

Can I merge files or remove background noise?

This studio works with one track. Use Merge audio to join multiple files. It does not include AI noise removal or claim to restore damaged recordings.
