Choose a template for your project

Build a portfolio gallery

Use the Expanding Panel Gallery to give each project a photo, a short description, and a link to the full case study. Replace the sample images in the demo, then check how the panels open on a phone and with a keyboard.

Show a product collection

Use the Progress & Depth Carousel for four products you want visitors to browse in sequence. Swap in your own photos and names, then check the progress indicator and how much of the next product is visible on a narrow screen.

Add social links with HTML and CSS

Try the Glassmorphism Social Media Buttons for five links or the Layered Social Buttons for three. Edit the destinations and accessible labels in index.html. The button effects use HTML and CSS. JavaScript switches the theme in the demo.

Once you have a starting point, follow the complete ZIP-to-website example to keep styles, scripts, and assets connected.
