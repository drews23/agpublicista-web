Where kinetic typography helps

Use a short animated title for a section change, word-by-word text to support a voiceover, or a cursor treatment in a software demo. For Reels and TikTok, test the message with audio muted so the text remains understandable on its own. Keep important figures or deadlines separate from longer paragraphs.

Movement should support hierarchy. A sound cue from the sound effects library can reinforce an entrance; it should not compete with speech. If you need a website treatment instead of a rendered video, explore the typography tool (Spanish) and animated wavy text tool (Spanish).

Exercise: one message in six seconds

These are suggested edit timings, not preset durations promised by the pack:

  • 0–2 seconds: “Give your titles rhythm.” Present one idea and let the entrance finish.
  • 2–4 seconds: “Change text and color.” Leave a stable reading interval. Split an overlong phrase before shrinking it too far.
  • 4–6 seconds: “Try the free pack.” Keep the closing message readable and clear of the cursor.

Export a test and watch it on a phone without audio. If you need to pause, extend the stable interval or shorten the message. Then check the voiceover version: text should not reveal information before the speaker explains it.
