Image Color Palette Extractor

Drop a photo or screenshot to extract its dominant colors in your browser. Use any extracted color in the generator to create a 50–950 shade scale, explore harmonies, check WCAG contrast, and export CSS or Tailwind values.

Your image

Extract a palette from a photo

Drop it here or click to choose · JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF, or SVG, up to 20 MB · analyzed in your browser, without uploading.

Extraction style

    Click a color to set it as the base, or drag its point over the photo for pixel-level adjustment. You can also move points with the arrow keys.

    Accessibility

    Contrast for each color

    Contrast ratios for each main color against white and black text, with WCAG 2.1 levels for normal text: 4.5:1 meets AA; 7:1 meets AAA.

      Turn color choices into usable design tokens

      Choose a base color or load a reference image. Inspect extracted colors, shade steps, and the displayed contrast values before exporting. Rare accents and generated external accents are labeled separately: a generated suggestion is not a color found in the original photo. Contrast checks apply to the selected pair, not to every possible layout.

      Are image files uploaded?

      The image analysis runs in this browser. Keep a copy of the original image; export only the colors and formats you need.
