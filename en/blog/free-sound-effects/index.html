10 futuristic whooshes and impacts

A whoosh helps the ear follow motion. Place it before the visual sweep finishes: its attack prepares the movement, while the impact can mark arrival. Panning makes sense when the image also moves.

Which pack fits the edit?

  • Visible cut: digital cuts or glitch.
  • Camera or object movement: whooshes or transitions.
  • Action result: game sounds.
  • Anticipation or reveal: suspense followed by an impact.
  • Brand entrance or exit: intro and fade sounds.

Always listen in the full mix. An isolated sample helps compare character; voice, music, and ambience determine whether it works in the finished video.

A practical workflow

  1. Mark the cuts, movements, and actions that need emphasis.
  2. Choose a sound family and try two candidates at each marker.
  3. Keep effects on tracks separate from voice and music.
  4. Align the attack or impact with the image, not automatically the beginning of the file.
  5. Adjust level and tail. Check headphones and a phone speaker.
