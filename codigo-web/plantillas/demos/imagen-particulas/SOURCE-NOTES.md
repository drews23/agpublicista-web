# Source notes / Notas de procedencia

## ImageGen prompt

> Wide cinematic landscape photograph for a 16:9 website hero background. An original alpine landscape photographed at blue hour, a serrated mountain range with snowy striations and a triangular summit on the RIGHT half, layered rocky ridges with subtle mist, dark blue-grey to deep petrol left half with low-detail smooth twilight sky and subdued far slopes reserved for white HTML headline, a thin warm amber dawn glow near right horizon and small cool turquoise accents in the mountain shadows. Full-bleed photographic detail, crisp granite and snow, atmospheric foreground valley, grounded natural realism, editorial film photography, rich darks with gentle local highlights for particles to sample, landscape horizon, sophisticated limited palette. Right ridge approximately 62–93 percent of frame width; generous quiet left half. No text, no typography, no watermark, no particles or glows, no people or objects. 16:9 landscape composition.

## Referencia visual / Visual reference

La estela toma como referencia el comportamiento de **Particle image**, de shubniggurath. Lienzo implementa el efecto en su propio motor WebGL. [CodePen](https://codepen.io/shubniggurath/pen/vEXwvXK)

The trail takes visual inspiration from **Particle image** by shubniggurath. Lienzo implements the effect in its own WebGL renderer. [CodePen](https://codepen.io/shubniggurath/pen/vEXwvXK)

## Paisaje / Landscape

`assets/hero-landscape.webp` se generó para Lienzo con ImageGen integrado. Muestra una cumbre alpina al anochecer, con el motivo a la derecha y un área despejada a la izquierda para el texto de portada.

`assets/hero-landscape.webp` was generated for Lienzo with built-in ImageGen. It shows an alpine peak at twilight, with the subject on the right and a quiet area on the left for hero text.
