Before opening the After Effects project

This is an editable .aep project inside a RAR archive. It is not a collection of .ffx animation presets or an installable .mogrt template. Extract it before opening after effect text animation pack.aep in Adobe After Effects.

  1. Keep an untouched copy of the download and edit a duplicate of the project.
  2. Read any version, missing-font, effect, or footage warnings. The guide does not establish a verified minimum version or complete plugin list.
  3. Choose a composition and replace its text. Adjust fonts before timing: a different font can change line breaks and width.
  4. Preview the entrance, stable reading interval, and exit. Export a short test to check clipping and readability at the final video size.

The original tutorial is in Spanish. Its chapters below identify where to adjust fonts, colors, duration, cursor colors, and export settings. The English guide explains the workflow; it does not change the video's language.
